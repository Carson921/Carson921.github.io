I contributed to the design of **UPPRESSO**, a privacy-preserving single sign-on protocol. I formalized the protocol and verified its authentication and privacy properties under the Dolev-Yao model, complemented by game-based cryptographic security proofs and reduction arguments.

The protocol was also adapted to a browser-only deployment setting by integrating the Proof Key for Code Exchange (PKCE) flow. {% cite guo2026uppressso %}
