I am a Computer Science Ph.D. student at the [University of Georgia](https://www.uga.edu/), advised by Prof. [Le Guan](https://guanle.org/). My research focuses on the **security and privacy of large language model systems and agents**, with particular interests in agentic browsers, prompt injection, long-term memory security, jailbreak attacks, and security evaluation.

Before joining UGA, I received my M.S. in Cyberspace Science and Engineering from the University of Science and Technology of China and my B.E. in Information Security from Huazhong University of Science and Technology. I was a Research Engineer Intern at Samsung Research America in summer 2026, where I worked on red-teaming, agent harnesses, and security benchmarking for agentic browsers.

My recent work includes **MetaBreak**, a systematic jailbreak method for online LLM services; **FragFuse**, a memory-based access-control bypass against LLM agents; and a systematic security analysis of emerging agentic browsers.

**I am available for internship opportunities from May to August 2027.**
